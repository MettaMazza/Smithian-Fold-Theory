# Naming Ledger — *The Big Unfolding* (and every book after)

The franchise speaks one language. Anything in the theory with no friendly name, or a name too cumbersome to say out loud, gets an accessible name here — coined once, used everywhere, never silently changed. A friendly name **never replaces** the real term on first contact; it walks in *beside* it (Video-Agent.MD law: the term and the analogy hold hands). After first contact, the friendly name carries the load.

**Living document.** Extended through Phase 1 as new concepts arrive. Every entry: friendly name · what it really is · anchor.
