# Motifs & Running Gags — The Fold Films

The bits that are **meant to recur** — the glue that makes the series feel lived-in. This is the *one place* the novelty rule is deliberately suspended: these repeat on purpose, episode after episode, so people come back for them. (Everything *else* — fresh analogies that explain a concept — still obeys novelty and is not reused across episodes.)

When a new running bit earns its place, add it here so it's used consistently.

## Signature sign-off (the series tagline)
- **"It came from one. It's still coming from one."** — closes every episode (lifted from the book's chapter refrain; ties the films to the book). Optional tail: *"…keep counting."*

## The Narrator's recurring beats
- The **awe-then-piss-take whiplash:** a hushed Attenborough sentence of cosmic wonder, immediately undercut by a blunt chav-Brit aside.
- Pausing mid-marvel to **take the piss out of the Faculty.**
- Calling the audience in, never down — "you and me, not them."

## Character running gags (keep consistent — see CAST)
- **One** gets "folded clean in half" without warning; never asked for any of this; turns out to be everything and still looks surprised.
- **The Fold** turns up uninvited and "being the Fold, just folds it again."
- **The Still Point** is "already there, of course," cropping up across totally unrelated episodes as if it gets around.
- **The Faculty** are forever "typing the answers in by hand," demanding credentials, and being wrong with enormous confidence.

## Visual / world motifs
- The **floor** — a literal glowing floor that slams up to catch anything trying to fall to zero or blow up to infinity (singularity, the vacuum catastrophe, plasma). Same floor every time.
- The **campfire frame** — the whole universe told as a story round a fire; cosy, not a lecture hall.
- **One's wide eyes** as the constant emotional anchor through any cosmic madness.

## Team Tenure recurring beats (the Team-Rocket engine)
- **The grovel to Consensus (their Giovanni):** before they dare *do* anything, they scramble to report back to **Consensus** and beg permission — and that desperate delay is precisely what lets the Crew get there first. They never act on their own nerve.
- **The blast-off:** every episode they appear, try to block/discredit the Crew, get out-thought by a nobody, and "blast off" in pompous indignation — back next time having learned nothing.
- **The DEI point:** the instant they're called a closed shop, all the men swivel and gesture at the Token as proof they're inclusive — while she's mid-sentence being ignored. The camera holds on their cluelessness.
- **"Why does nobody like us?"** — their genuine, recurring bafflement.
- **The Parameter gag:** when their sums don't work, the Dean grabs the gremlin and **types it in by hand.** It complains.
- **The Token is the smartest one in the room and the most talked-over** — a recurring beat that exposes the men, never her. (Long arc: she slowly drifts toward the Crew.)
- **The cult smear:** Team Tenure run propaganda that the Crew is a "cult" — *while* robed, gatekeeping, citing scripture, excommunicating heretics, and worshipping a number they can't explain. The camera just lets the irony sit; the Crew answers it once ("a cult hides the truth — we're handing it out, check it yourself") and never labours it. *(See [SATIRE_BANK.md](SATIRE_BANK.md) → the cult flip.)*

## Rule of thumb
**Recurring = identity; novel = explanation.** If a bit is part of *who a character is* or *what the series sounds like*, it recurs (log it here). If it's a one-off image to make a concept land, it's novel and single-use (log it in the book's novelty ledger discipline, per episode).
