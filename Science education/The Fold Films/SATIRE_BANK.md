# Satire Bank — The Fold Films

The reusable ammunition. Mine this for episodes. **North Star: South Park / Simpsons rule — fun first, but every laugh lands on something *satirically true*.** Punch up at institutions and the absurdity of modern times; never down at people or audiences. Everything the villains do should be **based in a real practice**, just dialled to eleven — that's what makes it bite.

## The core irony of "Consensus"
The name *is* the joke. **Consensus = agreement; truth = reality.** They are not the same thing, and history is a graveyard of "consensus" that was dead wrong: the Earth-centred universe, continental drift laughed out of the room, ulcers "obviously" caused by stress (until H. pylori), washing hands dismissed as nonsense (Semmelweis hounded to an asylum), quasicrystals "impossible" (Shechtman told to leave). The establishment's whole authority rests on a word that means *"what we currently all agree on"* — and they wield it as if it meant *"what is true."* Consensus is a headcount, not a proof.

## Consensus hypocrisies (the real targets — each a potential episode)
1. **"Trust the science"** — but science is a *method* (doubt everything, test everything); they've turned it into an *authority* (doubt nothing *we* say). They've inverted the one thing science is.
2. **"The debate is settled."** A phrase with no place in actual science, deployed precisely to *end* a debate they're losing.
3. **Peer review as holy writ** — sold as a truth-guarantee; actually unpaid mates rubber-stamping mates, catching almost no fraud, and rejecting anything too new to have friends.
4. **The replication crisis** — over half their headline results don't replicate, and the response is to carry on and call *outsiders* unscientific.
5. **Epicycles forever** — when a prediction fails, they don't drop the model, they bolt on another invisible patch (more dark-matter knobs, more parameters) and call it progress. The opposite of what they mock the ancients for.
6. **Funding the question, never the answer** — the apparatus is built to *measure* the unexplained constants forever, because the unanswered question *is* the salary. You can't think a thought that defunds you.
7. **Credentialism** — "where's your PhD?" used as a substitute for "is the maths right?" Ad hominem in a lab coat.
8. **Citation cartels & the h-index** — importance measured by who cites whom; a popularity contest cosplaying as merit.
9. **Publish-or-perish** — salami-sliced papers nobody reads, p-hacked to a magic threshold, all to feed a metric. Quantity worshipped, understanding optional.
10. **You pay to publish your own work** — the journals charge the authors *and* the readers, for research the public already funded. A tollbooth on knowledge.
11. **"Extraordinary claims need extraordinary evidence"** — applied selectively: bottomless skepticism for outsiders, a free pass for in-house dogma.
12. **Precision-worship** — twelve decimal places of a number they cannot explain, paraded as triumph. Measuring the mystery harder is not solving it.
13. **"You wouldn't understand"** — hiding behind complexity and jargon to dodge the *simple* question that would expose them.
14. **Science as an aesthetic** — the "I f***ing love science" merch, the celebrity-physicist brand, science as an *identity* to wear rather than a practice to do.
15. **"I don't know" treated as shameful** — the start of all science, recast as a weakness to bluff past.
16. **Heretic-cancelling** — defund, deplatform, "shown the door" (Shechtman, Semmelweis, every paradigm-breaker) — while claiming to *love* open inquiry.

## The cult flip (Team Tenure's propaganda vs. their own behaviour)
Team Tenure runs **propaganda calling the Crew a cult** — while displaying, beat for beat, the actual markers of one. The joke writes itself because **all of it is real practice**:
- **Robes and hoods** — they *literally* dress in ceremonial gowns and hoods.
- **Initiation rites** — the thesis "defence," a closed-door trial by elders before you're allowed in.
- **A priesthood** — peer reviewers, the anointed few who alone may bless or damn your work.
- **Sacred texts & a canon** — papers you *must* cite, scripture you may not contradict.
- **A secret language** — jargon as a wall to keep the laity out.
- **Tithes** — you *pay* to be published; you pay to read.
- **Excommunication** — question the doctrine, lose your funding, your post, your name.
- **Love-bombing the faithful** — prizes, citations, fellowships for those who conform.
- **A worshipped mystery-number** — 137, the magic number they bow to and cannot explain.
- **"Have faith in the experts."** — trust over test; the literal definition of faith.
- **Telling everyone else they're brainwashed.**
**The punchline, stated by the Crew once and never laboured:** a cult *hides* the truth and gates it behind initiation; the Crew *gives it away* and says *check it yourself.* The accusers are the cult. The "cult" are the only ones handing out the receipts.

## Long-arc satire payoffs (plant early, pay off across episodes)
- **The Token's slow defection.** The smartest, most-ignored person in Team Tenure drifts, episode by episode, toward the Crew — until she walks out. The institution's "diversity" prop turns out to be a real person the Crew actually valued. Lands the punch on the institution *and* earns a heart.
- **The watcher among them.** The narrator *is* the One, present through every adventure as the plain act of observation — so the show's deepest, truest 4th-wall thread is the slow, dawning sense that the voice telling the story is somehow *in* it, has been there the whole time, watching. Plant it in flickers; **hint, never state; never found.** (The universe observing itself, dressed as a storyteller who is quietly the origin.)
- **The Parameter gremlin's bid for freedom.** The free-parameter mascot, sick of being typed in by hand, wants *out* of the equation — a running B-plot that literally dramatises "zero free parameters."
