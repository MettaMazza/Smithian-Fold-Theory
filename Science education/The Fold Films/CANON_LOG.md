## Ep 00 — Pre-canon (the foundations, true before any episode)
Established by the Series Bible's world canon; every episode inherits these:
- There is **One** — the single number everything grew from. In the films One is **not a character**: it is the **origin and the narrator**, and it secretly accompanies the Crew through every adventure as the plain **act of observation** — present, hinted at, **never found or confirmed** (the show's deepest, never-stated thread: the narrator *is* the One).
- **The Fold** is the only move: double, and wrap the overflow round the back. It is the **mechanism** every discovery turns on — **not a character, not the narrator** (no hands, no slapstick). The One watches; the Fold moves.
- **Nothing can ever be Nothing** (the floor) — no zero, no negatives; nothing crashes to infinity or vanishes.
- **The Still Point** is ½ — its own mirror, folds straight home to One; turns up wherever balance is needed.
- The **Prime Families** (2, 3, 5, 7) are the forces; there is never a fifth family.
- The Fold only goes **one way** — which is why time has a direction.
- **Team Tenure** exist, are pompous, and are always wrong; they measure but never understand — and they cannot act without grovelling first to **Consensus**, the unseen boss they answer to. Named: **the Dean** (leader), **Crispin** (sycophant postdoc), **Priya** (the ignored, sharpest mind — slow defection arc), **Rupert** (PR/optics), and **the Parameter** (the hand-typed gremlin mascot).
- **The Crew** — a band of named humans — are **the protagonists**: each episode is their *adventure* to discover a truth of this universe in spite of academia, with **Team Tenure** as the recurring foil. Named: **Maria** (self-taught anti-hero) & **Matthew** (her tether), with **Nadia**, **Sol**, **Errol**, **Wee Danny**, and **Frances** (ex-Tenure). *Every member is lived, never labelled — see CAST.md.*
