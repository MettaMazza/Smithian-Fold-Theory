## Episode arc (template + surreal fold-world)
1. **Everyday open:** an old dry well by the fire; Danny dropping stones, no bottom coming.
2. **The why:** *"can you ever get to nothing? what's at the very bottom of everything — or does it just keep catching you?"*
3. **The shutdown:** the Dean *proud* — the singularity = a point of infinite density, "a profound mystery"; and **renormalisation** = "subtract one infinity from another, decades of Nobel sophistication" (the bucket boast). Crew don't back down: the Gardener — *"your great triumph is a trick for hiding [the infinities]… you put the holes in yourselves."*
4. **The way in (built-up):** the well; Danny's last stone *rings* on glass far below; the well widens into a great shaft; they fall — and the fall just *stops*, caught soft by the floor.
5. **The floor of light:** the bottom-of-the-bottom; no "below"; Danny can't break through (it catches him); the Gardener — *"there's no nothing for you to fall into."*
6. **Conflict + discovery (four monsters, one floor):** a tour of infinities the floor kills — the collapsing star's singularity (caught), the vacuum (calm on its floor), the oven (frozen notes), the fluid (capped) — **with a near-crush danger beat** (the collapse grabs the crew; the floor catches *them* too: *"it catches everything. it actually catches everything."*). Reframe: the no-nothing is a **mercy** — nothing falls to oblivion.
7. **Close on the gowns:** the Dean *"a profound and necessary feature"* (= please let the hole stay, I have a lovely bucket); *"In Defence Of The Beautifully Infinite"*; grovel to Consensus; blast off in *a cloud of bruised profundity.*
