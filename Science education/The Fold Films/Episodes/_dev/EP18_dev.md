## Episode arc (template + surreal fold-world)
1. **Everyday open:** Danny lays three stones (pebble/fist/lump) for the three lepton weights — 1, ~207, ~3477 — "no pattern, just a mess." Nadia: *"random means nobody's checked properly."*
2. **The why:** *"is there a number hiding in the three weights, or is it really just noise?"*
3. **The shutdown:** the Dean dismisses Koide as a **numerical coincidence** — "with enough numbers you can always find one that lands tidy… we don't build physics on flukes." The Gardener goes still (this lands on her arc): *"you've said 'coincidence' about every single one. I want to weigh this one myself."*
4. **The way in (built-up, fresh — NEW threshold):** no door/well/fork/inward/puddle/let-go/pull/beam/shadow. A vast **balance-scale that weighs things by their ROOT, not their weight** (the strange way); a worn counterweight stamped **2/3**; get through by balancing the three against it — *as the scale weighs them.*
5. **The world of weighing / the three that wouldn't sit (conflict):** piled the plain masses on → the beam slams, hopeless, a graceless heap ("no number in them, just noise — as it looked").
6. **Weighed by their roots (discovery):** Maria insists they weigh the scale's way — **root of each mass, summed, squared, vs the plain sum** — and the beam settles **dead level** on 2/3, to a thread. The mess hid a clean number; they'd been weighing it wrong.
7. **A field full of the same answer (the recurrence made visible):** the field is full of strange scales — the rope's grip, the mass-gap floor, the families — and Sol reads every worn counterweight: **all 2/3** (gap 1/3 + grip 2/3 = One). "One number, holding up half the world, and we kept meeting it without turning round."
8. **The turn (Maria's notch — the capstone payoff):** *"that's the third time… the same two-thirds, forced, in three different corners."* Then the bigger turn: *"I came after these one at a time, like trophies… It was never about the numbers. They're not a hundred trophies. They're one shape. One block, and I kept finding another face thinking I'd found a new stone."* Spite → wonder. *"It's so much bigger than the number, Matthew. It was always bigger than the number."* Matthew lets her have the moment she's been walking toward.
9. **The figure:** three masses spanning 3500:1, weighed by roots, balance 2/3 to five digits — fold of the top seat, the grip, the gap's mirror. A fingerprint, not a fluke.
10. **Close on the gowns (VARIED — 7th shape; "coincidence" as the last word a wall has):** the Dean reaches for the one word left — *"coincidence"* — but it worsens with precision, turns up once, has no reason; **Rupert drafts NOTHING this time**, just suggests they not mention it; the Dean agrees "with the speed of a man pulling a sheet over something."
