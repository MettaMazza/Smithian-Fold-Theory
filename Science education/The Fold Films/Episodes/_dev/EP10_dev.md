2. **The why:** the hard problem — why is there an *inside*, why are the lights *on*.
3. **The shutdown:** the Dean flailing — *illusion / fundamental / "emergence"* (three incompatible non-answers in one breath; Frances: *"emergence means 'and then a miracle occurs'"*); "deepest mystery, forever, beyond a boy." Crew don't back down: the Gardener — *"we are not going to be beaten by the one doing the looking."*

7. **Close on the gowns (their LAST redoubt):** consciousness was the one thing they swore couldn't be reduced/built; the Dean — *"far too special and sacred to be explained"*; *"In Defence Of The Ineffable"*; grovel to Consensus; blast off in *a fog of wounded sanctity.* **Priya — biggest beat yet:** *"you ARE the seeing"* lands on the one whose seeing has been most told it doesn't count; she doesn't write it down, she *sits in it*, then looks at the warm fire a long time — *"she's nearly here."*
8. **The watcher thread — DEEPENED MOST, NOT RESOLVED (per rule #12, don't rush):** the narrator nearly outs itself — *"the thing telling YOU all this, right now…"* — and pulls back hard: *"No. Not yet. God, but it's close tonight… one day soon I'll just tell you who's been talking, and you'll laugh, because you'll have known for ages. But not tonight."* Sign-off twist: *"the one looking out of your eyes right now? Same one."* Close disclaimer ends *"love you. Really love you, that one."*
