## Episode arc (template + surreal fold-world)
1. **Everyday open:** Danny notices the fire's **light** reaches the far trees but its **warmth** dies at his wrist — two reaches off one fire.
2. **The why:** *"is there a force that's mighty up close but can't get anywhere? What decides reach — it can't just be how hard you shout?"*
3. **The shutdown:** the Dean **thrilled** — weak force short-range because carriers heavy; the **mass gap** = "a literal million-dollar prize, unclaimed for 25 years," *"one does not reason out a million-dollar problem at a campfire — there are channels."* **Frances answers him** (see thread): *"there aren't channels, Gerald. There's just the answer, and the people too heavy to reach it. I was one."*
4. **The way in (built-up, fresh — NEW threshold):** no door/well/fork/inward/puddle/let-go/pull. They **step onto the beam of firelight** (the massless, infinite-reach carrier) and **ride the thing that reaches** out into the world.
5. **The country on tethers:** everything roped to everything; **tether length = weight turned inside out** (light → endless tether; heavy → short). Sol's reach-spine callback: lonely thread (weightless, reached the sky) vs rope (kept itself, reached nowhere) vs these (reach as far as they're light) — "same trick again."
6. **The messengers who couldn't reach (conflict):** the **weak carriers** (heaviest) strain at tiny tethers, snapping back a hair short of the far thing, forever-almost; Danny tries to carry one across, it's impossibly heavy. The Gardener: *"not weak — leashed. The only difference between it and the light is weight."*
7. **Hunting the weightless lump (the mass-gap floor):** Danny wants a weightless strong lump (infinite tether) — they hunt lighter and lighter lumps and **hit a floor**; a weightless lump would be a **zero**, forbidden (Errol: *"there's no nothing — same as the well"*). The smallest sits at **1/3**, the **Mirror of the 2/3 grip**, folding home to the One.
8. **The turn (Frances):** *"the short leash and the smallest lump are the same rule. Weight ties you down, and you're never allowed to weigh nothing."*
9. **The figure:** massless → forever; heavy → leashed to a speck; floor at 1/3 = Mirror of 2/3 → ONE. Forced; "a million-dollar problem at a campfire because it was never worth a million dollars — only ever true."
10. **Close on the gowns (VARIED — 5th shape; Consensus returns, fresh):** the Dean insists an answer reached *without the prize committee* can't count; Rupert drafts *"On The Proper Procedure For Being Right"*; **they go up to Consensus** — who **does not look up, does not rule, does not wave the bored hand** ("a truth that needs no committee is precisely the truth a committee has no use for"). Brings Consensus back after several rests, still unrevealed, fresh beat.
