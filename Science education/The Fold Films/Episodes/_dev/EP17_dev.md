## Episode arc (template + surreal fold-world)
1. **Everyday open:** Sol notices everyone casts **three nested shadows** (faint/middle/deep) — three of every person.
2. **The why:** *"why three of my shadow and not one or a hundred? Is there three of everything? And why does it stop at three — who shut the door after it?"*
3. **The shutdown:** the Dean **comfortable knowing the number and nothing else** — three generations measured exquisitely, but *why* three "the Standard Model does not answer and does not pretend to… we write three because we count three; there is no deeper reason." *"One does not ask the universe to justify its inventory."* The Gardener: *"'it's just given' is what you say when you've stopped looking."*
4. **The way in (built-up, fresh — NEW threshold):** no door/well/fork/inward/puddle/let-go/pull/beam. You **step into the deepest of your three shadows** (your heaviest self) and arrive **as three-of-you** — the same person at three weights.
5. **The world in triplicate:** everything in exactly three (light/middle/heavy), identical bar weight = the generations. Character beat: three Nadias agree and bristle; three Errols nod; three Matthews each watch a different Maria; Sol — "one of you, sounded out on three strings… the same note, played heavier."
6. **The hunt for the fourth (conflict — Danny's romance-of-more):** the three Dannys reason "if three, then a fourth, a fifth, a staircase forever" and hunt it; the world obliges with a literal staircase — **three steps and then a landing, no fourth step.** Danny gutted: *"why does it always stop? Why can't there just be more?"*
7. **The three seats / no fourth (discovery):** the Gardener — only **three steady seats** in the sector (quarter/half/three-quarters; middle = the Still Point); a fourth would need the **floor (zero, forbidden)** or the **ceiling (the One, unison — the home the three are FOR, not a generation)**. Set closed at three. **Not three because someone stopped counting — three because there are exactly three seats and the other two are taken by things that aren't generations.**
8. **The turn (Danny's notch):** *"it's not missing a fourth — it's finished. Three's the whole shape. The fourth I keep wanting would have to be a nothing or a home, and neither is a me."* The why-kid who's ached for MORE all series looks at a closed set of three and sees completeness, not a shut door.
9. **The figure:** 3 preimages, 3 standing notes, the 3 of space; measured 2.984. Forced, confirmed to a part in a hundred.
10. **Close on the gowns (VARIED — 6th shape; the "closed set defunds us" satire):** the Dean **cannot abide a closed inventory** — "we cannot declare the search concluded… a fourth may yet be found at higher energies… the funding for the search alone—"; the quiet part loud (an answer that says *there are no more* switches off the grant); Rupert drafts *"The Ongoing And Richly-Resourced Hunt For More."* **Crispin flicker:** trails after, glances at the three complete shadows, very nearly says so, then doesn't. No Consensus.
