## Episode arc (template + surreal fold-world)
1. **Everyday open:** Danny plays with his two hands — palms match, but a left won't lay on a right.
2. **The why:** *"why's there a left and a right at all? does the universe know — does it play favourites, or is it fair?"*
3. **The shutdown:** the Dean *serenely confident* — *"the universe is perfectly even-handed… to imagine it preferring a hand is childish."* The comic truth: that's what physics believed *until 1956*; the cosmos IS a southpaw; they won a 1957 **Nobel for the shock.** Crew don't back down: the Gardener — *"you won a prize for being amazed by something the universe was never hiding."*
4. **The way in (built-up, fresh):** no door — they go *backwards.* A **fork with no middle** (a clean Y, left/right, no straight-on); each must *pick a hand* (Errol decisive, Sol picks the kinder shape, Danny dithers, Nadia pulls him left); the fork seals behind them.
5. **The lopsided country:** a world of mirror-pairs, ½ apart, that match but never lay on each other; "near enough to fool you, never near enough to fit"; no even-handed thing anywhere, no straight line.
6. **The southpaw (conflict + discovery):** a weak force that *shakes every left hand and walks straight past every right* — and **blanks Danny** when he offers the wrong one (*"that's not fair… it only likes one hand"*). The turn (the Gardener): *"fair was never on offer — but it's not cruel, it's true. The favouritism is the reason you exist"* (every sugar in you twists one way; the neutrino's one wing; a fair universe would be a dead one). Danny offers his left; the force shakes it.
7. **Close on the gowns:** the Dean pivots shamelessly (*"well established, we proved it ourselves in 1957"* — forgetting he called it childish and that the prize was for *amazement*); *"On Symmetry, And Its Tasteful Violation"*; grovel to Consensus (perfect grey neutrality the universe was just shown not to have); blast off in *a flurry of rebranded hindsight.*
