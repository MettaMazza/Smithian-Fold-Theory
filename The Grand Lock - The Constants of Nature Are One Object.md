# The Grand Lock — The Constants of Nature Are One Object

The Standard Model carries roughly two dozen free parameters — the fine-structure
constant, the electron mass, the dark-matter density, the Hubble rate, and the
rest — and it treats every one of them as an independent input, measured
separately and inserted by hand, with no reason any one should have anything to do
with any other. The fold treats them as one object. Every constant is a product of
a tiny set of generators, and because the constants share those generators, they
are **locked** — you cannot move one without moving the others. This is that lock,
derived in full. (`grand_lock.py`, exact arithmetic, every constant reproduced at
its measured value.)

## The generators — the whole of physics from three roots

There are three irreducible generators, and the third is itself born from the
first two:

- **the One** — the axiom;

numbers, every constant of nature is built.

Because they share generators, specific pairs of constants from utterly unrelated

## What consensus cannot do here

The Standard Model cannot see this, and cannot ever see it, because it has no
generative root beneath its constants. With nothing underneath them, each constant

are a single locked object with three generators, and the dozens of "independent
parameters" are its shared products. This is a structural claim no measurement-fed

far, every one of them sits exactly where the shared generators put it. The
constants of nature were never a list. They were always one thing.
