# THE FOLD GO CAMPAIGN — the plan, registered before a line of engine code

The playbook order stands: the mathematics was made first, then the theory,
then chess — and Go is the next proof-in-development leg, run exactly the
way the chess campaign is run: zero parameters, zero axioms beyond the One,
every value counted or forced, pinned refereed protocols, gates before
rungs, autopsies before changes, negative results recorded raw. The same
desk, the same laws, a game the incumbents needed thousands of TPUs and
thirty million games to climb.

## Why Go is MORE native to the fold than chess was

1. **Go ends in a literal count.** Area scoring (Tromp–Taylor) is pure
   counting: a player's score IS the number of intersections they occupy
   or exclusively reach. There is no evaluation gap between "position
   value" and "counted quantity" to bridge — the game's own verdict is the
   fold's native object.
2. **The rules are reachability.** Liberties, capture, life — all of Go's
   dynamics are counted connectivity and counted reach over a lattice. The
   chess campaign had to derive piece values from board geometry; Go has
   no pieces to price. Geometry is everything, and geometry is counted.
3. **Superko is the closed orbit.** Positional superko is exactly the
   repetition law the chess bot already carries: a position already seen
   is a closed orbit, priced at the lock 1/2 — in Go it is simply illegal,
   an even cleaner form of the same law.
4. **Go endgames are combinatorial game theory** — the fold's certified
   home territory (the Grundy campaign's instrument passed its
   theorem-forced calibration exactly: nim's losing set's fold-spectrum
   supported exactly on the dual subspace). Berlekamp's mathematical Go
   endgame theory is counted game values; the fold reads that arithmetic
   natively.
5. **Influence is the halving law.** The proven fold-factor structure —
   influence decaying by 2^-d with distance, the same forced constant that
   carries the engine's session attention and that took the word-scale
   language gate (rung 5e) — is the natural counted influence field for
   Go: a stone's claim on an intersection halves per step of counted
   distance, summed exactly in rationals. No trained pattern, no learned
   shape: the one forced decay, everywhere.

## What transfers from the chess campaign, already proven

- **The search core**: budgeted iterative deepening, exact rational
  values, alpha-beta over counted evaluations, killer + HISTORY census
  ordering (every increment 1, no weights — lossless ordering bought
  depth 7 in chess), hard node budgets as powers of two.
- **The v19 parallel root**: root moves partitioned across worker
  processes (2^3 workers), exact single-core values, deepest-common-depth
  merge — built, smoke-proven, transfers unchanged.
- **The solved-class discipline (the KQKR pattern)**: solve small boards
  COMPLETELY in the fold's own arithmetic, certify in-room (every stored
  value re-proven by the room's own movegen against the value law; full
  census), bank the class, probe it at the root.
- **The referee law**: every match refereed by an INDEPENDENT rules
  implementation; every game logged in full from the first loss.
- **The ladder law**: pinned protocols (fixed games, both colours, varied
  openings), a losing run STOPS the ladder, losses go to the autopsy
  before any engine change, and no evaluation is ever iterated against
  match results — that is fitting by another name (the king-material
  lesson: two rules-grounded cures were gated, refused, and closed).
- **The gate law**: every engine version must beat its predecessor in a
  pinned head-to-head before it may face a rung.
- **The harness laws, learned twice**: referee games run in the MAIN
  process (engine bridges deadlock in pool children); one graded game
  owns the machine; per-game forensic flush; wall-clock alarms sized to
  the game, never to the load; long runs session-proof (nohup, chained).
- **The deflationary guardrails** (fold_chess/DEFLATIONARY_GUARDRAILS.md)
  bind every verdict in this campaign as they bind chess.

## The engine, organ by organ (all counted, nothing tunable)

- **Substrate**: ErnosPlain, compiled like the chess bot — the fold
  fights as itself (the BUILD LAW). One binary, stateless CLI protocol,
  the same worker argv mode for the parallel root.
- **Rules**: Tromp–Taylor exactly — area scoring, positional superko,
  suicide illegal. Logical rules chosen because they ARE the counted
  form of Go; komi is the referee's scoring convention, applied on the
  comparison side only, never inside the engine's mathematics.
- **State**: the board as a flat list (the language's law), groups and
  liberties as counted connected components, the seen-signature string
  for superko — the chess bot's own repetition machinery.
- **Evaluation, forced**: exact area count where the rules already decide
  ownership, plus the fold-factor influence field (2^-d per counted step,
  exact rationals) where they do not yet. Every constant is a generator
  quantity or a count; any candidate constant that cannot be traced is
  refused at review, the standing law.
- **Search**: the transferred core. Terminal positions score by literal
  Tromp–Taylor count — the evaluation and the verdict are the same object
  at the leaves, which chess never had.

## The rungs (each pre-registered when fired; verdicts recorded raw)

- **R0 — the census.** The rules engine's legal-position counts on tiny
  boards checked digit-for-digit against Tromp's published legal-position
  numbers (the perft analog; 1x1 through at least 3x3), plus a
  random-game referee harness vs an independent implementation: N games,
  every move and the final count cross-checked, zero disagreements or the
  engine does not proceed.
- **R1 — the solved class.** Solve 2x2 and 3x3 COMPLETELY (retrograde,
  exact), certify in-room (full census, every value re-proven), and check
  against the published perfect-play results. The KQKR discipline on Go's
  own soil.
- **R2 — 5x5.** Published perfect play is known (B+25, opening at the
  centre under area rules): the engine's deep search must find the known
  first move and hold the known result line against the referee. Solve as
  far into 5x5 as the method carries (the board-completion ambition,
  restated for Go).
- **R3 — the 7x7/9x9 ladder.** Pinned refereed matches vs public
  opponents in ascending strength: GNU Go's levels, then Pachi at fixed
  playouts, then a capped KataGo. The chess ladder's stop rule and
  autopsy law apply verbatim.
- **R4 — 13x13, then 19x19.** The same ladder, the same laws, the full
  board. Entry is earned by R3, never scheduled.
- **In parallel, the science leg**: the chess campaign's spectral results
  (sparse fold-spectrum of solved value fields, AUC ~0.998 fragment
  generalization) re-measured on Go's solved small boards — is the Go
  value field loud in the fold basis the way chess endgames and trained
  weights are? Pre-registered instrument, the Grundy calibration standard.

## What is NOT in this plan

No neural network, no playouts-as-training, no pattern databases, no
tuned constants, no komi inside the engine, no evaluation iterated
against results, and no timescale projections — rungs are earned in
order, and completion is the author's call, nobody else's.
